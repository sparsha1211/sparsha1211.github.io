Getting started

  1. Open WarpDeck once after installing — Android does not deliver boot events to a never-opened app.
  2. In Settings, choose your Boot countdown app and pick your Quick Launch Top 5 apps.
  3. Set the boot delay and countdown timer, make sure Launch on boot is on, then reboot and put the headset on.

Troubleshooting

The countdown did not appear after reboot

  • Open WarpDeck at least once after install or an update, and do not force-stop it (force-stopped apps do not receive boot events).
  • Check Settings → Launch on boot is enabled and a boot app is selected, then try Settings → Simulate boot / Test countdown.
  • Put the headset on: the countdown waits for headset focus and shows “Waiting for headset to be ready…” until you can see it.

Boot timing feels early or late

  • Adjust Settings → Boot delay (0–60s) and Countdown timer (0–30s). Total boot-to-launch is delay + countdown once the panel is focused.
  • For exact delay timing, grant “Alarms & reminders” when WarpDeck prompts; without it, timing is approximate.

Reset WarpDeck or delete your data

  • Uninstall WarpDeck, or go to system Settings → Apps → WarpDeck → Storage → Clear storage. All preferences are stored only on your device — see the Privacy Policy.

Privacy

WarpDeck collects no data, has no network access, and stores preferences only on your headset. Read the WarpDeck Privacy Policy by Bearcat Inc.
