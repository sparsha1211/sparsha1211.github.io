Boot straight into your favorite game.

Pick an app, put the headset on — WarpDeck counts down and launches it. No digging through menus, no scrolling the library. Boot. Countdown. You're in.

How it works

1. Pick your boot app

Choose any game or app installed on your Meta Quest or Android-based VR headset as the auto-launch target, and pin your Top 5 favorites.

2. Reboot and put the headset on

After a short settle delay, the countdown appears — only when you're actually wearing the headset, so it never ticks away unseen.

3. You're in

Do nothing and your app launches. Tap Cancel anytime to land on your Quick Launch favorites instead.

Features

Quick Launch panel

Your Top 5 apps in big, controller-friendly tiles. One tap launches instantly. Browse and search everything installed.

Make it yours

Boot delay (0–60s), countdown timer (0–30s), panel refresh-rate preference, keep-awake, and live headset status (refresh rate, battery, thermal state).

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no network access. WarpDeck requests no INTERNET permission and your settings never leave the headset. See our Privacy Policy.

About

WarpDeck (com.warpdeck.launcher) is a lightweight 2D panel utility — about 2 MB — for Meta Quest / Horizon OS and other Android-based VR headsets.
